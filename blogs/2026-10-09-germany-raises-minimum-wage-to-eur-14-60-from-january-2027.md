The German federal government has enacted a EUR 14.60 hourly minimum wage, effective January 1, 2027, in the Fifth Minimum Wage Adjustment Ordinance (MiLoV5). The government signed it on November 5, 2025, and published it in the Federal Law Gazette (BGBl. 2025 I Nr.
