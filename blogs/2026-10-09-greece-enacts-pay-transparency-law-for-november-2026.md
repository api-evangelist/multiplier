Greece has transposed the EU Pay Transparency Directive, Directive (EU) 2023/970, through Law 5316/2026, published in the Government Gazette on July 6, 2026. The Greek Ombudsman is the equality and monitoring body. The Labour Inspectorate enforces the rules.
